# Image asset guidance

The current site uses remote Pexels editorial photography to establish a premium visual direction while client-approved imagery is being prepared.

## Selected editorial directions
- Hero: Black woman, natural curls, soft neutral light.
- Hair: natural texture / lived-in colour direction.
- Beauty: brown-skin facial / calm towel-wrap setting.
- Nails: neutral nail styling and diverse hands.
- Supporting imagery: warm salon and neutral beauty editorial references.

## Production replacement
Replace editorial imagery with buyer-owned or client-approved photography before presenting the gallery as a portfolio of Maggie's completed work.

Keep:
- explicit image dimensions;
- `srcset` and `sizes`;
- descriptive, non-deceptive alt text;
- deliberate `object-fit` / cropping;
- existing image replacement points.

Do not introduce fake client transformations or AI-generated client work. Record retained third-party image provenance and re-check licensing before handover.
